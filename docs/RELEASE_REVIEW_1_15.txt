NOT VERIFIED; DO NOT CLAIM 10/10 CERTIFICATION
- This PHP runtime has no PDO SQLite/MySQL driver, so live DB migration and
  actual multi-user integration were NOT tested here.
- End-to-end live phone -> director -> customer WebRTC not device verified.
- Browser visual screenshot check was blocked by ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ADMINISTRATOR;
  no workaround attempted.
- Actual camera-audio mixing, browser MediaRecorder output and HostGator
  large-file uploads have not been verified on real devices.

LIMITATIONS
- Pilot P2P fan-out: 6 concurrent viewers maximum; host browser must remain
  open and online. Internet bandwidth grows with each viewer.
- WebRTC signaling uses PHP and ICE/STUN; TURN relay is not provisioned.
  Additional TURN settings may be needed for strict NAT/firewall combinations.
- The program is transmitted only to logged-in members by live.php; it is not
  automatically published to public Watch.
- Live Director lists its own approved QR phone connections, not unapproved
  private cameras from other models' accounts.
- Director records Program canvas plus selected host microphone; individual
  camera tracks/audio are not archived as separate cameras by this feature.
- A recording is saved into the Director's private gallery and linked to the
  ended/live program. Save/backup behavior should be checked on the host.
