AZHOTTIE STUDIO v1.22.2 — LIVE VIDEO DELIVERY REPAIR
Date: 2026-11-21

Symptoms reported on v1.22.1
- Phone is approved, actively sharing and displaying live preview FPS.
- Admin selects Broadcast now, but a viewer sees a frozen first Program frame.
- Director may show 'The on-air camera stopped delivering video.' when its own
  decoder is throttled by a hidden or background tab.

Defects addressed
1. Director rendered the outgoing canvas exclusively on requestAnimationFrame.
   Browser hidden-tab throttling can stop captured Program frames even though
   the incoming phone WebRTC connection remains active.
2. The fan player tried to autoplay a WebRTC stream containing an audio track
   without muting it. Autoplay may be denied after asynchronous ontrack.
3. Each audio/video ontrack event reset video.srcObject, risking playback interruption.
4. Incoming phone preview FPS was the only visible frame activity indicator;
   there was no measurement of actual outgoing Program frames to fans.

Changes
- Hidden Director tab: limited background Program render timer; fan video is
  relayed directly from the explicitly chosen single phone track, without a
  new peer/room. Selected camera cannot be inferred from a mere phone connection.
- Per-viewer RTCRtpSender.getStats() samples outbound video frame counts; stalled
  video can fail over to the selected phone on a per-viewer basis.
- Fan starts video muted for autoplay compatibility; explicit Unmute sound
  button allows audio on viewer gesture. Audio track arrival does not replace
  or restart the video MediaStream.
- Fan decoded frame monitor shows when a connected transport has no new frames.
- Do not take the on-air camera off Program simply because its HTMLVideoElement
  paused decoding while the Director was hidden. Actual mute/end still stops it.
- Local video track routing is serialized across hide/show/Take transitions.

Known limits / safety
- A direct camera video relay omits Program canvas overlays and transitions for
  that viewer. Normal canvas Program resumes when the Director returns visible;
  a measured sending stall may keep a viewer on direct relay until reconnect.
- Browser background throttling is not solved for multi-camera compositions,
  prerecorded Program media or complete overlay-preserving browser recordings.
  Keep the Director tab visible and phone screens awake during these workflows.
- User access remains the same: only authorized members can join and only
  an explicitly taken approved phone is eligible for a direct video relay.
- No SFU: the existing peer-to-peer member capacity and network limitations remain.
- No storage or database schema changes.

Release checks
- npm test: 355/355 JavaScript assertions passed.
- Deployment PHP syntax: 113/113 files passed.
- Chromium fixture: delayed audio track did not replace the video stream;
  muted playback produced >50 decoded frames in local test.
- Production validation remains necessary: separate device/network viewer,
  visibility hide/show, audio unmute, composed overlay and recording, camera
  disconnection, TURN relay, and actual MySQL/signaling.
