AZHottie Studio v1.32.0 — Cash Pickup & Local Delivery by Product

Scope and user behavior
- Every product has an optional, individually configured "Cash payment & local fulfillment" section inside Commerce > Products > Create/Edit. Controls include enable/disable cash orders, pickup availability/location/hours/instructions, delivery availability, supported ZIP codes and state, delivery fee, minimum merchandise subtotal, and delivery instructions. Cash-only active products require no Stripe account or external seller URL.
- A shared Commerce > Local Orders tab lists requests for staff. It DOES NOT contain global pickup/delivery settings. Each request is locked to the product's price, fulfillment method, ZIP fee/minimum, and approved creator attribution at creation time.
- Customers can request cash at pickup or cash on local delivery from Shop, shoppable creator Watch videos, or the Live viewer when the particular product offers it. No online cash handling occurs. A private receipt code permits order-status checks and cancellation before staff acceptance.
- Pending requests are NOT paid or accepted. Staff quote final tax and total first, then explicitly accept, prepare pickup/dispatch delivery, confirm physical cash collection, and confirm fulfillment. Full cash refunds require explicit staff confirmation plus a reason, and reverse estimated creator commissions. Cancelled/unpaid orders never produce commissions.
- Product-specific fulfillment terms use a configured state and exact five-digit ZIP allowlist; no automatic distance/geocoding or carrier integrations are implied. Pickup addresses are provided only by the product owner; buyers are instructed not to arrive before confirmation.
- Delivery phone, name, email, street address and notes are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from the private app_secret. Only an authenticated administrator can reveal customer contact details in Local Orders. Model records, Agent Brain, Chat, History and public receipt/status endpoints exclude customer PII.
- The existing notification bell/History/Agent Chat receive deduplicated cash request, collection, refund and staff action events; the existing private hourly follow-through cron flags stalled cash requests. No new notification subsystem is introduced.
- Signed Stripe-paid orders, manual external-provider orders and staff-attested physical cash orders remain distinct. Cash product-subtotal commissions start only when staff confirm collection. Product subtotal excludes delivery fees and tax from commission calculations. Cash reports are not mislabeled payment-provider verified.
- Schema 37→38 creates per-product settings and encrypted cash-order records. Existing products default to cash OFF. Old Stripe/external-seller functionality remains unchanged. Migration is replay safe and preserves existing data.
- Cash confirmation is an audit event by a staff member, not proof of physical exchange by AZHottie. Sales tax must be calculated and entered by an authorized human; this release does not implement automated sales-tax services, fulfillment logistics, refunds processing, payouts, or SMS/email sending.
- Required gates: PHP lint, SQLite lifecycle, product-specific settings/revision tests, private receipt/authorization tests, cash collected/fulfilled/refunded sequence, role isolation, responsive browser fixtures and archived deployment ZIP; live hosted MySQL and actual local operation remain separate production-verification gates.

Deployment
1. Stop active studio operations and back up complete MySQL/SQLite data, website files, private config and media.
2. Merge the new full deployment package into the existing application tree, preserving private storage, uploaded files and storage/config.php. Do not rerun install.php.
3. Verify database reports schema 38; in Commerce > Products, enable a product's cash pickup and/or local delivery, including pickup address/hours or delivery ZIP fees and minimums.
4. Test two different products with conflicting cash settings to confirm isolation. Place pickup and delivery requests, confirm receipt access and permissions, quote tax, accept, collect cash, fulfill, cancel, refund, and review the unified Agent notifications and model commission estimates.
5. Confirm the existing hourly Agent follow-through cron runs. Verify production delivery operations and cash/tax compliance before enabling real customer orders.
