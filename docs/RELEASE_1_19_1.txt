VIDEO TIMELINE — WHAT CHANGED
-----------------------------
1. V1 clips have dedicated trim-start and trim-end handles; drag handles to
   non-destructively change the edit. The existing Clip inspector retains
   numeric in/out points and Mark In/Mark Out controls. Snap applies to trim.
2. Right-click any V1 clip for clip actions such as trim here, split at
   timeline position, duplicate, mute/unmute source sound, detach sound to A2,
   or open the inspector. The context menu appears near the pointer.
3. Right-click a volume line in A1 source-audio or A2 detached-audio track to
   add a volume keyframe. Drag each point vertically to change volume;
   adjacent points are linearly interpolated. A context menu offers remove
   keyframe and segment-level actions. Saving retains keyframes (up to 32
   per audio segment) and applies them in browser preview/export.
4. Detach moves the source sound into an independent A2 audio clip; the V1
   original's sound is muted to avoid doubling the mix. A2 has independent
   timeline start/trim/volume, can be dragged along the timeline and opens
   its own inspector. Removing a detached clip does not change the original.
5. Both Clip and A2 audio inspectors are floating and draggable over the
   monitor/timeline; the edit layout no longer confines them to one column.
6. Edits are source-ID based and owner scoped; raw private media URLs never
   become persisted project references. Original footage stays intact.

LIVE DIRECTOR — PHONE FEED STATUS / CONNECTIVITY
------------------------------------------------
1. Director now receives whether an approved phone has an active, fresh live
   offer and whether the host answered it. An inactive card explains the
   required next action: share video on the phone, check connection, or
   configure TURN where needed. Local phone recording alone is not a feed.
2. Phone and host peers use the same configured iceServers from the server
   rather than different hardcoded ICE settings. This does not provision TURN:
   operators must supply working relay credentials when networking requires it.
3. On WebRTC interruption, temporary disconnects get a short grace period;
   failed/expired sessions are identified and bounded retries can re-offer.
   Local phone recording is unaffected by network reconnection attempts.
4. Live Director QR invited phones go through approved paired sharing even
   if the person scanning the QR is signed into the same site on the phone.
5. Audio sent from a phone remains separately opt-in and subject to Director
   mixer controls. Camera preview remains private until Director takes it.

INSTALLED-SITE SMOKE SEQUENCE
-----------------------------
1. Take a complete backup. Stop and end existing live broadcast/recording.
2. Deploy the FULL_DEPLOY archive into the existing application directory
   without replacing existing storage data or running install.php.
3. Hard refresh /my-editor.php and /live-director.php; confirm new controls.
4. Open a private saved timeline, test trim, separate A2, automate volume and
   reopen project; export and listen for the expected gain changes.
5. In /live-director.php, invite + approve a phone; on the phone open the
   scanned /my-camera.php URL under HTTPS. Start camera then Share live OR
   check Live preview on Record and start a recording.
6. Wait for 'Live camera connected to Director' on the phone and moving
   video in the Director's LEFT-side phone tile. Check that selecting it with
   'Broadcast now' puts the intended source in Program without exposing
   private unselected thumbnails to viewers.
7. Confirm with a second browser/fan account and listen for audio; record a
   short show and confirm replay publication works as intended.
8. On a remote/cellular network, use configured STUN/TURN. Device approval
   by itself is not proof the camera stream was negotiated/decoded.
