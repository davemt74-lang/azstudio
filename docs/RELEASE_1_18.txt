AZHOTTIE STUDIO 1.18.0 — LIVE DIRECTOR RELIABILITY
====================================================

BUILD SCOPE
- Persistent, moving per-camera thumbnail video elements. Preview/Take never
  replaces the thumbnail HTMLVideoElement or resets its WebRTC stream.
- Health states use decoded frames via requestVideoFrameCallback, with
  advancing-media-time fallback where not supported. Report startup, live,
  muted, blocked autoplay, no new frames, and disconnected states.
- Do not allow Take or auto-switch into a black/stalled/unready feed. Stop
  broadcasting a failed source; show holding slate in manual switching mode.
  When automatic switching is enabled, choose another healthy source.
- Local camera diagnostics identify browser permissions, HTTPS, camera device
  count, unsupported APIs, and common device-use errors. Camera device changes
  refresh device discovery. All cameras are explicitly opened by the Director.
- Program audio: continuous host mic channel with level/mute and meter, plus
  an independent selected-camera channel with level/meter and explicit enable.
  Camera audio follows the Program source, not Preview. On a guest phone the
  extra microphone must be explicitly opted in; permission audio=false by
  default. Phone audio never plays out from the thumbnail speakers.
- Private saved Director scenes: camera Preview reference, mode, transition,
  titles, overlays. Recall does NOT take a camera to Program; operator presses
  Take. Scenes are per Director account (12 max).
- Uploaded image overlays: PNG/JPEG/WebP under owner-scoped protected storage;
  up to 4 MiB and 4096x4096 pixels. Image opacity is baked into media via
  canvas drawing. Overlay media endpoint requires a signed-in editor/admin.
- Recording: Program canvas and audio mix are captured as one stream. Verify
  server-reported file byte count before attaching to a saved live show or
  discarding the local download backup. Retry preserves upload token/media ID.

PRIVACY AND OPERATIONS
- No original private camera stream is sent directly to a fan. Only Program
  canvas + destination audio is sent. A new phone feed enters Preview only.
- No new database migration. User-private scene/image assets use separate
  storage/live-director/<owner-id>/ files with 0700 folders and 0600 files.
- No third-party cloud storage or AI service needed for this update.
- HostGator supports only low-fan-out WebRTC until a streaming relay/SFU exists;
  the six-viewer cap is intentionally unchanged.

TESTS AND NOT-YET-VERIFIED
- New JS camera-health/role/audio/scene/record tests plus baseline suite.
- New PHP scene storage tests plus PHP syntax checks and existing live contract.
- Local Chromium returned ERR_BLOCKED_BY_ADMINISTRATOR for the preview fixture,
  so physical moving frames, actual iPhone-to-Director media, stored media,
  visible camera layout, and audio/video output were NOT browser-certified.
  These are final HostGator acceptance gates before a verified 10/10.

SMOKE TEST (on actual HTTPS installation)
1. Click Find cameras; open a laptop camera and USB camera. Confirm both tiles
   show moving pictures and LIVE VIDEO, not just a connection label.
2. Preview camera B privately, Take camera A, then switch while fan watches.
3. Scan QR with unlogged-in phone. Approve, tap Share live, and confirm a third
   moving thumbnail; switch to it only after operator presses Take.
4. With no camera selected confirm holding slate; simulate USB disconnect and
   confirm Program no longer shows a stuck last frame.
5. Set host microphone, inspect level, mute, and adjust volume. Opt in phone
   microphone and enable camera audio; verify fan hears only desired mix.
6. Upload transparent PNG, enable overlay, save scene, recall it, Take preview.
7. Record 20 seconds while switching and talking, stop, verify saved recording
   and preview it in Admin -> Saved Live Productions before publishing.
